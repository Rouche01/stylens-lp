The quickest way to get help is via email. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign in?

GoStylens uses passwordless authentication. To sign in, simply enter your email address in the app, and we'll send you a one-time verification code (OTP). You can also sign in instantly using your Google or Apple account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

GoStylens subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. To cancel, please open your device's subscription settings and follow the standard cancellation process for your platform.

Is my data private?

Yes, your privacy is our priority. Your photos and style data are encrypted and used only to provide personalized style recommendations. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

App Store Support

If you are experiencing technical issues with the GoStylens mobile app, please ensure you are running the latest version. If the problem persists, please email us with your device model and a brief description of the issue.
