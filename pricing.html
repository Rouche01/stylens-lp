Pricing FAQ

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your Core subscription at any time.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. Free Preview includes 7 days of unlimited sessions after signup, then 5 sessions every month.

What happens after the 7 days?

You stay on Free with 5 sessions every month. Upgrade to Core anytime for unlimited sessions.

What counts as a session?

A session is one styling chat. On Free, each session includes up to 20 messages and 10 images.

What’s the difference between Free and Core?

Free gives you a 7-day unlimited start, then 5 sessions a month. Core unlocks unlimited sessions, saved looks, and style memory.

Do I need a credit card for Free?

No. You can start Free Preview without adding a card.

Is yearly billing cheaper?

Yes. Core yearly is $79.99, about $40 less than paying $9.99 monthly for a year.
